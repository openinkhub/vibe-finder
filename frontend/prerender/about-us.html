About Me

Hi, I’m Rekha. First, a little about me: I’m an expert makeup artist and teacher based in Bangalore. In fact, I have 14+ years of experience in bridal makeup, makeup artistry and beauty education.

But beyond makeup, I believe that the best makeup is the kind that still feels like you. Over the years, I’ve worked with brides of each kind. Each one, however, had her own personality and her own idea of the perfect wedding look. Some know exactly what they want. For example, some come with a folder full of Instagram inspiration. And some simply say, “Rekha, I trust you. You tell me what will suit me.” I love all three.

My Approach to Bridal Makeup

As a Bridal Makeup Artist in Bangalore, my approach is simple: I listen first. I know your features, skin, own style, comfort level and the look you have in mind. Then I guide you towards what I believe will work best for you.

I’ll always give you my honest opinion. But I’ll never push you into a look just because it is trending.

Your wedding makeup should not make you look like someone else. It should make you look like the most confident version of yourself.

My Makeup Philosophy

Less is more. Makeup has no rules. If you like it, wear it.

For me, makeup is not about following a fixed formula. It is about understanding the person, the event and the result they want. Then I create a look that feels comfortable, confident and personal.

More Than Bridal Makeup

My journey with makeup does not stop at Bridal Makeup. I also teach new makeup artists through expert courses and hands-on training. As a result, they build the skills and confidence to work professionally.

For women who simply want to do their own makeup better, I run self makeup and self grooming classes. The focus is on understanding what really works for you rather than blindly following trends.

I will listen, guide you honestly and help you decide what feels right for you. You do not need to have everything figured out before you contact me.
