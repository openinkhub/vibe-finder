2.1 About Our Services

We provide professional makeup artistry using HD and Airbrush techniques, along with makeup training courses and personal shopping assistance. Our makeup services cover:

  • Bridal Makeup — including Pre-Wedding, Sangeet, and Wedding Day makeup, typically preceded by a personalised consultation covering your vision, skin type/condition, outfit, jewellery, venue, and timing, with on-site assistance on the day of the event.
  • Engagement Makeup — for engagement ceremonies and related pre-wedding functions.
  • Non-Bridal Makeup — for members of the bridal party/family (e.g., bridesmaids, relatives) attending wedding-related events.
  • Party Makeup — for birthdays, anniversaries, and other social events.
  • Editorial Makeup — for magazine, shoot, and portfolio-style looks.
  • Fashion Makeup — for runway, fashion shows, and creative/avant-garde looks.

We use premium makeup products from established brands (such as MAC Cosmetics, Bobbi Brown, Estée Lauder, HUDA Beauty, Lancôme, Urban Decay, and Anastasia Beverly Hills, among others) and follow standard hygiene practices, including sanitising tools, brushes, and accessories before use.
