2.1 About Our Services

We offer expert makeup in HD and Airbrush, as well as makeup courses and personal shopping. Our makeup services cover:

  • Bridal Makeup, for pre-wedding, sangeet and wedding-day looks. It usually starts with a consult about your ideas, skin, outfit, jewellery, venue and timing. On the day, we work with you on site.
  • Engagement Makeup, for engagement functions and related pre-wedding events.
  • Non-Bridal Makeup, for the bridal party and family, such as bridesmaids and relatives, at wedding events.
  • Party Makeup, for birthdays, anniversaries and other social events.
  • Editorial Makeup, for magazine, shoot and portfolio looks.
  • Fashion Makeup, for runway shows and creative looks.

We use premium products from known brands, such as MAC Cosmetics, Bobbi Brown, Estée Lauder, HUDA Beauty, Lancôme, Urban Decay and Anastasia Beverly Hills, among others. We also follow standard hygiene steps, including cleaning tools, brushes and accessories before use.
