About This Portfolio

This makeup portfolio shows real work by Rekha across weddings, events, shoots and classes. Each photo was made for one person, one outfit and one setting. So use it to judge her skill and range, not as a menu of fixed looks.

The scroll section above shows how one face moves from bare skin to a full look. Then the gallery below groups the work by type. You can also open a service page to see more photos of one kind of look.

Bridal Looks

Bridal work is the heart of this portfolio. You will see soft, glowing skin for morning muhurthams. You will also see deeper, richer looks for evening receptions.

Notice how the makeup works with heavy temple jewellery, silk sarees and jasmine. The base stays even, and the eyes stay clear even in wide group photos. For more, see South Indian bridal makeup and the bridal makeup service.

Engagement and Reception Looks

Engagement makeup is often lighter than the wedding look. However, it still needs to last through photos, rings and greetings. Reception looks, on the other hand, can be bolder, with more shine and a stronger lip.

Look for how the colours match the outfit. A pastel lehenga needs a softer face, while a deep red saree can carry more depth. You can see more on the engagement makeup portfolio.

Party and Family Looks

Not every client is the bride. Mothers, sisters and guests want to look polished too, but in a quieter way. So these looks focus on fresh skin, neat eyes and colours that suit the event.

Party looks range from a soft day look to a glam night look. For more examples, see party makeup and non-bridal makeup for South Indian events.

Fashion, Editorial and Shoot Work

Shoot makeup has a different job. It must work under studio lights, flash and close-up lenses. Also, it must match the brief, the clothes and the mood of the story.

Here you will find clean beauty shots, bold colour and graphic eyes. Some looks are made for a single frame, while others must last a full day of changes. See the fashion and wedding makeup and pre-wedding shoot makeup pages for more.

Groom Looks

Grooms want to look fresh, not made up. So groom makeup evens out the skin, calms shine and tidies the brows. The result looks natural in person and clear in close-up photos.

Look at how the groom's skin matches the bride's in couple shots. That balance matters, because the two of you will stand side by side in most frames. See groom makeup for more.

Work From Classes

Some photos come from Rekha's classes. In those, she shows one step at a time, such as a cut crease or a long-wear base. Students then repeat the step on a model.

These photos show what you can learn to do yourself. If that interests you, look at the self grooming classes or the makeup courses.

How These Photos Help Your Booking

Pick two or three photos that feel close to what you want. Then share them when you send your date and venue. Rekha will explain what will work on your skin and in your light.

In short, the photos start the talk. The final look is always planned for you. When you are ready, send a request or read client reviews.

Also, the makeup portfolio grows through the year. So check back before your consult to see the newest bridal and party work.

When you look at a photo, think about your own day. Ask yourself if the light, the outfit and the mood are close to yours. If they are, that photo is a good one to share. If not, it can still show a detail you like, such as a lip shade or a soft eye.

Common questions

Are these photos of Rekha's own work?

Yes. The portfolio shows makeup done by Rekha for clients, shoots and classes.

Can I ask for a look from the portfolio?

Yes. Share the photo when you book. Rekha uses it as a guide and adapts the look to your face, skin and outfit.

Will my makeup look exactly like the photo?

Not exactly. Each face, outfit and light is different, so Rekha plans the look for you.

Which brands does Rekha use?

Rekha uses trusted brands such as MAC Cosmetics, Bobbi Brown and Estée Lauder.
