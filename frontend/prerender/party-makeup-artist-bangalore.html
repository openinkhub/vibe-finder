How We Care About Your Desired Makeup

Your preferred makeup direction should be the starting point. Tell Rekha what you want to look like, what you normally wear, what you are comfortable with and what you do not want. Reference photographs can help communicate a finish, but they are not a promise that an identical look should be copied. The final application is adapted to your facial features, skin, outfit, jewellery, hairstyle, lighting, photography and the practical demands of the occasion.

Do's and Don'ts

  • Share the party type, timing and venue.
  • Bring or share outfit and jewellery details.
  • Tell the artist how much definition you normally enjoy.
  • Discuss any skin sensitivities.
  • Allow enough time for makeup and the included simple hairstyle.
  • Select heavy makeup solely because the event is in the evening.
  • Ignore the colour and neckline of the outfit.
  • Introduce new skincare products before the event.
  • Arrive significantly later than the agreed appointment time.
  • Assume a reference look will suit every face in exactly the same way.

Care Before & After Your Makeup

Before: prepare the outfit and accessories and communicate the event timing. If the event is photographed professionally, mention it. Keep your normal skincare routine stable before the appointment.

After: avoid unnecessary touching and protect the hairstyle while dressing and travelling. Remove makeup thoroughly after the event.

Service Scope and Planning

The exact service plan should be confirmed according to the occasion, number of people, location, ready time, outfit changes, waiting requirements and any additional styling requested. Where an item is not listed as included, it should be discussed and confirmed before the appointment.
