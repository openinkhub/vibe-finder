How We Care About Your Desired Makeup

Your own idea of the look is the starting point. Tell Rekha how you want to look and what you often wear. Also say what feels at ease and what you don't want. Sample photos help show the finish you like. However, the look will not be an exact copy. Instead, Rekha adapts it to your features, skin, outfit, jewels, hair, the lighting and the day itself.

Do's and Don'ts

  • Share the party type, timing and venue.
  • Bring or share outfit and jewels details.
  • Tell the artist how much definition you normally enjoy.
  • Discuss any skin sensitivities.
  • Allow enough time for makeup and the included simple hairstyle.
  • Select heavy makeup solely because the event is in the evening.
  • Ignore the colour and neckline of the outfit.
  • Introduce new skincare products before the event.
  • Arrive significantly later than the agreed booking time.
  • Assume a sample look will suit each face in exactly the same way.

Care Before & After Your Makeup

Before: prepare the outfit and accessories and communicate the event timing. Also, tell Rekha if you have booked a photographer. Keep your normal skincare routine stable before the booking.

After: avoid needless touching and protect the hairstyle while dressing and travelling. Remove makeup fully after the event.

Service Scope and Planning

Confirm the plan before the day. That means the event, the number of people, the place and the ready time. Also agree on outfit changes, waiting time and any extra styling. Also, if something isn't on the included list, agree on it before the booking.
