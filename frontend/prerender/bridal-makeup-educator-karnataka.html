Editorial Makeup Portfolio

Above all, editorial makeup is a chance to explore makeup as visual style. This portfolio presents looks created for editorial, creative and image-led work where concept, styling, features and photos come together. Editorial makeup may involve stronger colour, texture, shape, definition or bold beauty choices depending on the creative plan.

So the brief drives the approach, not one daily formula. The portfolio demonstrates the ability to adapt makeup to visual concepts and work with styling, fashion, photos and creative plan. Each editorial look can require a new balance between makeup, hair, wardrobe, lighting and the intended image.

Explore the Work

Browse the portfolio to see new looks, finishes and styling. If you like a look, bring it to your consult as a sample. However, it will not be an exact copy.

Plan Your Look

At the consult, talk through the event, your skin, outfit and jewels. Then decide how bold you want to go. Overall, the goal is a look that suits you and the event.

Common questions

What is editorial makeup?

Editorial makeup is makeup created for visual storytelling, creative concepts, fashion images or image-led photos.

Can editorial makeup be more daring?

Yes. Depending on the creative brief, editorial makeup can explore stronger colour, shape, texture or definition.

Can I share a creative mood board?

Yes. A mood board or visual sample can help communicate the intended plan.

Does editorial makeup depend on photos?

Photography and lighting are key considerations when planning an editorial look, along with styling and the creative brief.

What Students Learn From These Looks

Each photo here also works as a lesson. Students study how the base was matched, how the eyes were shaped and how the look holds up in close-up. Rekha uses work like this in class to show cause and effect.

For example, a student can see how a small change in contour changes the whole face. She can also see why a bridal base needs more prep than a party base. In short, the portfolio turns theory into something you can see.
