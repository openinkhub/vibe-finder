First, share your event date, venue, event and the makeup service you are looking for. That way, Rekha knows your needs and can suggest the right service.

Looking for Bridal Makeup?

For bridal requests, share your wedding date, venue, functions, and preferred makeup style. You can also read about bridal makeup first. Then Rekha can talk through the complete makeup plan for your wedding events.

Not Sure Which Makeup Service You Need?

However, you do not need to know exactly which service is right for you. Tell us about your event and desired look, and we can help you spot the right option.

Tell Us How You Want to Look

Your makeup should suit your features, outfit, event, and own style. Tell us whether you prefer a natural, soft glam, classic, defined, or more creative look.

How Rekha Approaches Your Makeup Requirement

Rekha first knows your needs, features, skin, comfort, event, and expectations. The aim is to create a look that feels like you while being professionally planned for the event.

What Happens After You Contact Us?

After your request arrives, Rekha reviews the details, checks open dates and replies with the next steps.

For Brides: Plan Before the Wedding Day

Bridal makeup is easier to plan when you talk early. Share your wedding details early, so Rekha can plan makeup, hair, styling and timing around your functions.

Your Comfort Matters
