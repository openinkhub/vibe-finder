Planning guide

What to have ready before your consultation

A well-prepared consultation means less back-and-forth and a clearer picture of your day — which lets us focus on the creative part.

  • Event dates & times
    List all ceremonies with start times so we can plan the makeup timeline.
  • Number of people needing makeup
    Bridal party, mother of the bride, sisters — give us a rough count early.
  • Theme, palette & outfit colours
    Share references for each outfit. Makeup should complement, not clash.
  • Venue type & lighting
    Outdoor natural light vs banquet hall vs flash photography — it changes everything.
  • Skin type & concerns
    Oily skin, sensitivity, allergies, previous reactions — your artist needs to know.
  • Trial date preference
    Minimum 4–6 weeks before the wedding, ideally during your pre-wedding shoot.

Bridal makeup timeline

Start your makeup 2–3 hours before the ceremony — longer for elaborate looks or if bridal party makeup is included.
