How We Care About Your Desired Makeup

Your preferred makeup direction should be the starting point. Tell Rekha what you want to look like, what you normally wear, what you are comfortable with and what you do not want. Reference photographs can help communicate a finish, but they are not a promise that an identical look should be copied. The final application is adapted to your facial features, skin, outfit, jewellery, hairstyle, lighting, photography and the practical demands of the occasion.

Do's and Don'ts

  • Share the mood board or creative brief before the shoot.
  • Share the outfit, designer references and lighting plan where available.
  • Discuss the number of looks and required changes.
  • Communicate the intended audience or portfolio purpose.
  • Allow enough time for makeup adjustments between looks.
  • Arrive without a clear understanding of the creative direction.
  • Assume one beauty look works for every garment.
  • Add multiple unplanned look changes without allowing time.
  • Make last-minute skincare changes.
  • Separate makeup planning from hair, wardrobe and photography.

Care Before & After Your Makeup

Before: the creative team should ideally align on wardrobe, hair, makeup, lighting and shot list. Skin should be prepared with a familiar routine. If multiple looks are planned, schedule sufficient transition time.

After: remove makeup thoroughly after the shoot and return to a gentle skincare routine. If the shoot involved long wear or repeated changes, give the skin time to recover.
