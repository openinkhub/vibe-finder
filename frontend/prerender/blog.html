What You Will Find Here

This journal shares hands-on tips from Rekha's work with brides, clients and students. You will find skin prep advice, wedding timelines, product tips and answers to common questions.

Each post is short and easy to follow. So you can read one before your trial, your shoot or your next class. The guides below go deeper on one topic, such as choosing a bridal makeup artist or learning self grooming.

Where to Start

If you are a bride, start with how to choose a bridal makeup artist in Bangalore. Then read about makeup trials and skin consults.

If you want to learn, read about the self grooming classes and the makeup courses. Also, the portfolio shows how the tips look in real work.
