Bridal Makeup Booking

Premium bridal makeup booking works best when you share the details early. That means your date, venue, functions, skin needs, outfit, jewellery, photo needs and the finish you like. Then Rekha plans her on-site makeup around the agreed wedding-day timings.

Before you book, compare the bridal makeup service, engagement makeup and bridal makeup packages. You can also read how to choose a bridal makeup artist in Bangalore.

Bridal Booking Do’s

  • Give a full and correct date, venue, timing and list of services.
  • Tell Rekha about skin sensitivities, allergies, past reactions or recent skin treatments.
  • Share the look you want and your key styling needs in advance.
  • Keep a clean, well-lit area ready for makeup at the venue.
  • Be ready at the agreed start time.
  • Tell Rekha early about any change to the venue, timing, services or number of people.
  • Ask about makeup for family or guests in advance, where you can.
  • Keep jewellery, clothes, accessories and valuables safely away from the makeup area.

Bridal Booking Don’ts

  • Don't assume a request holds your date. Rekha confirms the date only once she receives the agreed advance.
  • Don't start new skincare, facials, peels or waxing just before the wedding, unless your skin expert advises it.
  • Don't arrive late or delay the agreed prep time without telling Rekha.
  • Don't assume last-minute extra services will be possible.
  • Don't expect a trial to give the exact same result on the wedding day, because conditions can change.
  • Don't leave valuables unattended near the makeup area.

Bridal Makeup Booking Terms & Conditions

  1. 1.Who These Terms Cover

    These terms apply to bridal and on-site makeup bookings. When you confirm a booking and pay the advance, you accept these terms.

  2. 2.Booking Confirmation

    Your booking is confirmed only once Rekha receives the agreed advance. Then the date and time are held for you alone. Until then, the date stays open for other requests. Rekha takes the advance off the final amount, and you pay the balance on or before the service date, as agreed. Once your booking is confirmed, Rekha will not take another bridal booking for the same date.

  3. 3.Your Date Is Held for You

    The advance is a promise to hold the agreed date and time. However, the cancellation and date-change terms below still apply.

  4. 4.Cancellation by Client

    The advance is non-refundable, because the date is held for you alone. A cancellation covers the whole agreed package, including makeup, hair styling and other services. Rekha does not refund amounts paid. In rare cases, she may make an exception at her discretion.

  5. 5.Date Change

    You can ask to move your booking to a new date. However, a change is not guaranteed and depends on whether Rekha is free. If she is free, she can move the booking once you both confirm. If she is not free, the request counts as a cancellation, and the advance stays non-refundable. You pay any extra service, travel or other costs the change causes.

  6. 6.Makeup Trial

    A trial is optional and paid. It cannot promise the exact same result on the wedding day, because skin, lighting, outfit, jewellery, weather and photo conditions can change. Big changes on the day may need extra time and may not always be possible.

  7. 7.Wedding-Day Timings

    You must be ready at the agreed start time. Delays from you, the venue, the photographer, family, outfit changes or other causes can cut the time left for makeup. A long delay may shorten or change the service. Extra waiting time may cost extra.

  8. 8.Local Travel

    Travel charges may apply. Rekha shares local travel, parking, toll and transport costs at booking. You must give correct venue details. You pay any extra costs a venue change causes. For early-morning bookings, you may need to pay for private transport.

  9. 9.Outstation Bookings

    For outstation bookings, you pay travel costs unless the booking clearly includes them. You also arrange, or pay back, an overnight stay. It should be safe, clean, private and close to the venue. Extra travel caused by a change of venue or timing may cost extra.

  10. 10.Venue Needs

    Also, please give a clean, well-lit and airy space for makeup, with a good chair and a table where needed. The space should allow work without breaks, with reliable power where needed.

  11. 11.Skin Details

    You must tell Rekha about known sensitivities, allergies, past reactions and recent skin treatments. Please don't start new skincare, facials, peels or waxing just before the wedding, unless a skin expert advises it. Rekha takes reasonable care, but she cannot promise that no skin reaction will happen.

  12. 12.Extra Services

    Rekha provides only the services agreed in your booking. Extra makeup, hair changes, touch-ups, or family and guest makeup may be possible if time allows. If so, they cost extra. Rekha does not have to accept a request that would upset the agreed timings.

  13. 13.Extra People

    First, you must share and confirm the number of people who need services in advance. Last-minute requests depend on whether Rekha is free, and they are not guaranteed.

  14. 14.Touch-Ups

    Your package includes touch-ups only when it says so, or when you book them separately. If Rekha stays beyond the agreed time, extra charges may apply.

  15. 15.Products and Personal Items

    Rekha uses expert products and tools that suit the agreed service. In turn, you must keep jewellery, clothes, accessories and valuables away from the work area. Rekha is not responsible for loss of or damage to items outside her direct control.

  16. 16.Photos and Social Media

    Rekha may ask to photograph or film your finished look. She uses images only with your consent. If you don't want images shared publicly, please say so before the event.

  17. 17.Events Outside Our Control

    Rekha will make every reasonable effort to complete your booking. If serious illness, an accident, an emergency or other events outside her control stop her, she will try to arrange a suitable replacement artist, if one is free. If no replacement is offered, her liability is limited to refunding what you paid for the service she could not provide.

  18. 18.Changes to Services or Package

    Please share any change to the services, number of people, venue, timing or package in advance. Changes depend on whether Rekha is free, and they may change the fee.

  19. 19.Final Payment

    Finally, you must pay the balance by the agreed date. If an agreed payment is not made, Rekha may hold back completion of the service.

  20. 20.Communication and Acceptance

    Please choose one main contact for key booking messages. Share key changes in writing. Paying the advance confirms that you accept these terms. The terms in force on your booking date apply to your booking, unless you both agree otherwise in writing.
