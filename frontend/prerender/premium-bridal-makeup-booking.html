Bridal Makeup Booking

Wedding makeup planning works best when your date, venue, functions, skin needs, outfit, jewels, photo needs and preferred finish are discussed in advance. Rekha provides tailored bridal and on-site makeup services planned around the agreed wedding-day timings.

Before you book, compare the bridal makeup service, engagement makeup and bridal makeup packages, or read how to choose a bridal makeup artist in Bangalore.

Bridal Booking Do’s

  • Provide complete and accurate wedding date, venue, timing and service details.
  • Inform the artist about skin sensitivities, allergies, reactions or recent cosmetic procedures.
  • Communicate your preferred makeup look and key styling needs in advance.
  • Keep a clean, well-lit and right makeup area ready at the venue.
  • Be ready at the agreed start time.
  • Inform the artist early about changes to venue, timing, services or number of people.
  • Discuss extra services for family members or guests in advance whenever possible.
  • Keep jewels, clothing, accessories and valuables safely away from the makeup station.

Bridal Booking Don’ts

  • Do not assume a request reserves your wedding date. The date is confirmed only after the agreed advance is received.
  • Do not introduce new skincare, facials, peels or waxing at once before the wedding unless recommended by your skincare expert.
  • Do not arrive late or delay the agreed prep timings without informing the artist.
  • Do not assume last-minute extra services are automatically available.
  • Do not assume a makeup trial guarantees an identical wedding-day result because conditions may differ.
  • Do not leave valuables unattended around the makeup station.

Bridal Makeup Booking Terms & Conditions

  1. 1.Applicability

    These Terms & Conditions apply to bridal and on-site makeup bookings. Confirming the booking and paying the advance constitutes acknowledgment and acceptance of these terms.

  2. 2.Booking Confirmation

    A booking is confirmed only upon receipt of the agreed advance. The advance secures the artist’s open dates exclusively for the agreed date and time. Until the advance is received, the date remains available for other requests. The advance is adjusted against the final amount. The balance is due on or before the service date as agreed. Once confirmed, the artist will not accept another bridal booking for the same date.

  3. 3.Exclusivity of the Date

    The advance represents a commitment to reserve the agreed date and time and is subject to the cancellation and date-change terms below.

  4. 4.Cancellation by Client

    The advance is non-refundable because the date is reserved exclusively for the client. Cancellation applies to the entire agreed service package, including makeup, hairstyling and other services. No refund will be provided for amounts paid. Exceptional consideration may be provided at the artist’s discretion.

  5. 5.Date Change

    A request to change the booking date may be considered if dates are free and is not guaranteed. If the new date is offered, the booking may be transferred after confirmation. If unavailable, the request is treated as a cancellation and the advance remains non-refundable. Any difference in service, travel or other expenses resulting from the change is payable by the client.

  6. 6.Makeup Trial

    A makeup trial is optional and chargeable. A trial does not guarantee an identical wedding-day result because skin condition, lighting, outfit, jewels, weather and photos conditions may vary. Substantial changes requested on the wedding day may require extra time and may not always be possible.

  7. 7.Wedding-Day Schedule

    The client is responsible for being ready at the agreed start time. Delays caused by the client, venue, photographer, family members, outfit changes or other circumstances may reduce available makeup time. Significant delays may shorten or modify the service. Extra waiting time may be chargeable.

  8. 8.Local Travel

    Travel charges may apply. Local travel, parking, tolls and transportation costs will be communicated at booking. The client must provide accurate venue details. Extra costs caused by a venue change are borne by the client. Early-morning bookings may require private transportation at the client’s cost.

  9. 9.Outstation Bookings

    For outstation bookings, travel expenses are borne by the client unless expressly included. Overnight accommodation is to be arranged or reimbursed by the client and should be safe, clean, private and reasonably close to the venue. Extra travel caused by venue, timings or venue changes may be chargeable.

  10. 10.Venue Needs

    The client should provide a clean, well-lit and ventilated area good for makeup, with a good chair and table where needed. The area should allow uninterrupted service and reliable power where required.

  11. 11.Skin Details

    The client must inform the artist about known sensitivities, allergies, reactions and recent cosmetic procedures. Clients are advised not to introduce new skincare, facials, peels or waxing at once before the wedding unless recommended by a skincare expert. Reasonable care will be taken, but no guarantee can be made that no skin reaction will occur.

  12. 12.Extra Services

    Only services agreed and included in the booking will be provided. Extra makeup, hairstyle changes, touch-ups, family or guest makeup may be accommodated if time and open dates permit and will be charged separately. There is no obligation to accept requests that compromise the agreed timings.

  13. 13.Extra Persons

    The number of people requiring services must be communicated and confirmed in advance. Last-minute requests are if dates are free and are not guaranteed.

  14. 14.Touch-Ups

    Touch-ups are included only when specifically stated in the package or booked separately. Staying beyond the agreed service period may result in extra charges.

  15. 15.Products and Personal Belongings

    Professional products and tools right to the agreed service will be used. The client is responsible for keeping jewels, clothing, accessories and valuables away from the work area. The artist is not responsible for loss or damage to belongings not under the artist’s direct control.

  16. 16.Photography and Social Media

    The artist may request permission to photograph or record the completed look. Images will only be used with the client’s consent. If the client does not want images used publicly, this should be communicated before the event.

  17. 17.Unforeseen Circumstances

    The artist will make reasonable efforts to fulfil the booking. If unable to provide the service because of serious illness, accident, emergency or circumstances beyond reasonable control, reasonable efforts will be made to arrange a good replacement, if dates are free and suitability. If no replacement is offered, liability is limited to refunding amounts paid for the service not provided.

  18. 18.Changes to Services or Package

    Changes to services, number of people, venue, timing or package should be communicated in advance. Changes are if dates are free and may result in a revised fee.

  19. 19.Final Payment

    The remaining balance must be paid within the agreed timeline. Where an agreed payment is not received, the artist may withhold completion of the service.

  20. 20.Communication and Acceptance

    The client should nominate one primary contact for key booking communication. Key changes should be communicated in writing. Payment of the advance confirms acceptance of these Terms & Conditions. The terms applicable on the booking date govern the booking unless otherwise agreed in writing.
