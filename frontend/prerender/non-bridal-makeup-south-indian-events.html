Non-Bridal Makeup Portfolio

Not every special occasion needs a bridal makeup look. This portfolio showcases makeup created for clients who want polished, occasion-ready beauty without traditional bridal styling. Explore makeup designed around personal features, occasion, outfit, comfort and desired finish.

The work ranges from soft, simple makeup to bolder occasion looks. It all depends on the client and the event. Non-bridal makeup is about adapting the artistry to the person and situation. A daytime event, a family function, a work event or a dinner each needs a different balance. For example, the skin, eyes and lips may change.

Explore the Work

Browse the portfolio to see different looks, finishes and styling. If you like a look, bring it to your consultation as a reference. However, it will not be an exact copy.

Plan Your Look

At the consultation, talk through the occasion, your skin, outfit and jewellery. Then decide how bold you want to go. Overall, the goal is a look that suits you and the event.

Frequently asked questions

What does non-bridal makeup include?

Non-bridal makeup refers to occasion-focused makeup that is not specifically planned as bridal makeup.

Can I request a natural non-bridal look?

Yes. Tell Rekha how much coverage, eye definition and finish you like.

Is non-bridal makeup suitable for family functions?

It can be suitable for a range of celebrations and occasions, depending on the event and the look you want.

Can I bring a reference image?

Yes. Reference images can help communicate the style and finish you prefer.
