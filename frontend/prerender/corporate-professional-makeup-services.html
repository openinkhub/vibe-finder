Why it matters

Cameras see what eyes miss

A camera sensor picks up shine, uneven pigmentation and colour casts that the human eye naturally smooths over. Professional makeup corrects for the specific lighting temperature, sensor profile and compression of your shoot before you're ever in front of the lens.

The result is images where your skin looks like you — not washed out, not shiny, not orange under warm tungsten. Just polished.

Long-wear formulas

Products selected for 8–12 hour performance without touch-up — critical for all-day shoots.

HDTV-calibrated

Colours and contours tuned to broadcast-spec lighting so faces read naturally on screen.

Flash & strobe-safe

Foundations and powders that don't bounce light into white in controlled studio environments.

Skin-first philosophy

Prep, hydration and base work means skin looks like skin, not like makeup — even at 4K.
