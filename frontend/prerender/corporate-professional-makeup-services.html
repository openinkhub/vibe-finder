Why it matters

Lenses see what eyes miss

After all, a lens picks up shine, uneven colour and colour casts that the eye smooths over. So Rekha adjusts the makeup for the light and the lens before you step in front of it.

The result is images where your skin looks like you — not washed out, not shiny, not orange under warm tungsten. Just polished.

Long-wear formulas

Products chosen to last 8–12 hours without touch-ups, which matters on all-day shoots.

HDTV-calibrated

Colours and contours tuned to TV lights, so faces look natural on screen.

Flash & strobe-safe

Bases and powders that don't flash white under studio lights.

Skin-first philosophy

Prep, hydration and base work means skin looks like skin, not like makeup — even at 4K.
