How We Care About Your Desired Makeup

Your own idea of the look is the starting point. Tell Rekha how you want to look and what you often wear. Also say what feels at ease and what you don't want. Sample photos help show the finish you like. However, the look will not be an exact copy. Instead, Rekha adapts it to your features, skin, outfit, jewels, hair, the lighting and the day itself.

Do's and Don'ts

  • Provide the creative brief and visual samples.
  • Discuss the photographer, lighting and intended publication or portfolio use.
  • Share wardrobe and styling details.
  • Discuss the number of looks and any major transitions.
  • Allow enough time for detailed creative application.
  • Start an editorial shoot without agreeing on the concept.
  • Assume editorial makeup should look like party makeup.
  • Add major concept changes at the last minute without revising the timings.
  • Ignore the relationship between makeup and lighting.
  • Use a sample image without explaining which elements you want to retain.

Care Before & After Your Makeup

Before: align the creative team on the concept, wardrobe, hair, makeup, lighting and shot list. Prepare the skin with a familiar routine. If the shoot needs special textures or big makeup changes, allow extra time.

After: remove editorial makeup carefully after the shoot. Return to a gentle skincare routine and avoid needless irritation from repeated cleansing or rubbing.

Service Scope and Planning

Confirm the plan before the day. That means the event, the number of people, the place and the ready time. Also agree on outfit changes, waiting time and any extra styling. Also, if something isn't on the included list, agree on it before the booking.

Common questions

What is Editorial Makeup?

It is makeup designed around a creative or editorial visual concept rather than a standard social event.

Does Rekha tailor Editorial Makeup?

Yes. Editorial Makeup is customisable and priced on request.

What should I provide before an editorial project?

Share the concept, mood board, wardrobe, lighting, photographer and number of looks, so Rekha can plan the makeup around them.

Can Editorial Makeup be bold or unconventional?

Yes. You can agree the scope based on the concept. Then agree the final plan with the creative team.

How is editorial makeup different from party makeup?

Party makeup suits a social event. Editorial makeup, however, tells a creative story through makeup and photos.
