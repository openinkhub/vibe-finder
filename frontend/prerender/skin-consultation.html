Know Your Skin Before Choosing Your Makeup

Good makeup starts with understanding the skin under it. A Skin Consultation looks at your skin today and any concerns. It also shows how they may affect your makeup.

Why Choose a Skin Consultation?

Different skin types and concerns can influence makeup prep, finish, comfort, and longevity. Knowing your skin before a big event means a better plan, with no last-minute changes.

Why Now?

Don't leave skin prep to the last minute before a wedding or event. Maybe your skin is dry, oily, sensitive or uneven. If so, talk about it early, so you have time to prepare.

How It Helps

  • What your makeup may need from the skin
  • Areas requiring extra prep

Common questions

Why do I need a skin consultation before my wedding?

It helps Rekha know your skin type, so she can plan your prep and choose products that last all day.

Is the skin consultation a medical treatment?

No. It is makeup advice only. For skin problems, please see a dermatologist.

How early should I have a skin consultation?

Two to three months before the wedding is ideal. Then you can settle into a steady skincare routine.
