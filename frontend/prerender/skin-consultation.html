Understand Your Skin Before Choosing Your Makeup

Good makeup starts with understanding the skin it is being applied to. A Skin Consultation focuses on your current skin condition, concerns, preparation needs, and how these factors may affect your makeup experience.

Why Choose a Skin Consultation?

Different skin types and concerns can influence makeup preparation, finish, comfort, and longevity. Understanding your skin before an important event allows your makeup plan to be more considered rather than relying on last-minute changes.

Why Now?

Skin preparation should not be treated as a last-minute step before a wedding or event. If you have concerns about dryness, oiliness, texture, sensitivity, uneven appearance, or makeup sitting differently on certain areas, discussing them early gives you more time to prepare.

How It Helps

A Skin Consultation is particularly useful before weddings, major celebrations, photoshoots, or any event where you want your makeup preparation to be planned rather than rushed.
