How to Pick the Right Service

Rekha offers a full range of makeup services in Bangalore. Each card above opens a full page with prices, what is included and common questions. If you are the bride, start with bridal makeup. For a ring ceremony or roka, see engagement makeup.

Family members and guests usually book non-bridal makeup. On the other hand, a birthday or office event is better suited to party makeup. For shoots and creative projects, there is editorial, fashion and photoshoot makeup.

HD or Airbrush: A Quick Guide

Both give a smooth, even finish. HD makeup uses brushes and sponges, and it suits most skin types. Airbrush makeup sprays a thin layer, so it feels light and holds well in heat.

Rekha helps you choose at your consult. Also, the HD makeup and airbrush makeup pages explain the difference in more detail.

What Every Service Includes

Each booking starts with a short chat about your skin, outfit, jewellery and event. Then Rekha preps the skin, builds the base and finishes the eyes, lips and lashes. She uses trusted brands and cleans every tool before use.

Hair styling and saree or lehenga draping are included. Extra touch-ups and family makeup can be added, so tell Rekha what you need when you send your request.

Also, if you need makeup for more than one person, list them all in one request. Then Rekha can plan the order and the time each look needs, so no one is rushed on the day. In short, one clear list saves time for everyone.

Finally, if you are not sure which service fits, just tell Rekha about the day. She will point you to the right page and the right look. Then you can book with a clear plan.
