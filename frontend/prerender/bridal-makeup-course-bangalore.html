Bridal Makeup, In Depth

The programme focuses entirely on the skills required to create expert bridal makeup. You learn bridal skin prep, long-lasting and photography-ready skin tone, bridal eye artistry, lashes, lips, face sculpting and complete bridal looks.

You also learn to work with many skin tones, face shapes, eye shapes and ages. Then you adapt your method to each bride.

What You Will Learn

  1. 01
    Bridal Skin Prep & Skin tone
    Bridal skin prep, skin types, priming and colour correction. Also foundation matching, light to full coverage, long-wear methods and setting for photos.
  2. 02
    Bridal Face Artistry
    Concealing, highlighting, cream and powder contouring, face-shape-based sculpting, blush placement, bronzing, highlighting and creating dimension without heaviness.
  3. 03
    Bridal Eye Artistry
    Eye shapes and bridal eyeshadow placement. Also soft, classic and glam bridal eyes, smokey eyes, shimmer, liner and kohl.
  4. 04
    Brows, Lashes & Details
    Bridal brows and natural brows. Also how to choose, trim, layer and apply lashes, plus mascara and finishing.
  5. 05
    Bridal Lip Artistry
    Lip prep, lip shapes, lining and overlining. Also bridal lip combinations, matte to glossy finishes, long wear and custom colour mixing.

The Bridal Look Lab

The emphasis throughout is adaptation because no two brides have the same face, skin or idea of beauty.

  • Classic Bridal
    Timeless, elegant and photograph-friendly.
  • Soft Glam Bridal
    Fresh, luminous and modern.
  • Traditional Bridal
    Stronger definition while complementing classic bridal styling.
  • Modern Glam Bridal
    More defined eyes, sculpted skin tone and elevated glamour.

Bridal Consultation

Before the makeup begins, you need to know the bride. You learn to run a bridal consult and know what the bride wants. You also learn to read the event and lighting, handle samples, run a trial and create a personal bridal look.

Bridal Longevity

A bridal look has to survive more than a photograph. The course covers long-wear makeup, layering, and handling humidity, oil and sweat. It also covers setting, touch-ups and makeup that looks natural in person and great in photos.

Complete Bridal Makeup

Through guided practice, you bring each step together: skin prep, base, sculpting, eyes, lashes, brows, lips and setting. The goal is to move from knowing individual steps to understanding the complete bridal look.

What Makes This Different?

We don't teach one bridal look. We teach you how to read the bride and create the right look for her.

A bride may have different skin, features, age, preferences, lighting, jewels and expectations. The expert artist’s job is to know how to adapt. That is the skill this course builds.
