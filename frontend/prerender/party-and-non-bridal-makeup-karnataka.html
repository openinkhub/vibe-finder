Party and Non-Bridal Makeup for Guests, Family and Events

Of course, not every celebration needs a bridal look. Instead, party or non-bridal makeup is for the mother of the bride, sisters, friends and guests. It also suits receptions, festivals, anniversaries and parties across the state.

In this guide, you'll see how non-bridal makeup differs from bridal, and how to choose the right finish. Rekha's work for brides is on the bridal makeup in Bangalore page.

Party vs Non-Bridal Makeup

Party makeup is typically makeup with a simple hairstyle for an evening event. Rekha's party makeup starts from ₹8,000. Non-bridal makeup is a fuller service for family at weddings. It includes makeup, lashes, hair and saree or lehenga styling, from ₹10,000 per person per session.

See party makeup and non-bridal makeup for the details.

Choosing the Right Look

  • Match the intensity to the event: soft for daytime, more definition for evenings.
  • Let the bride's look lead; family looks should suit, not compete.
  • Consider age, comfort and how long you will wear the makeup.
  • Share your outfit colour and jewels so the look ties together.

Learn to Do It Yourself

If you attend many functions, learning your own makeup saves time and money. The self-grooming classes teach event makeup that suits your features.

Common questions

What is non-bridal makeup?

It is makeup for family and guests at weddings and events. It often includes hair and outfit styling, but it is lighter than a bridal look.

How much does party makeup cost?
