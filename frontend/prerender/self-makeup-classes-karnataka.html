Self Makeup Classes in Karnataka: What You Learn

Learn your own face, not someone else's: what self makeup classes teach and who they are for.

By Rekha Krishnamurthy · 19 November 2026 · 3 min read

Why Take Self Makeup Classes?

Lessons show you someone else's face. These classes teach you about yours. You learn your features, your skin and what really works for you. That is the difference between copying a look and owning it.

Rekha runs small self-grooming classes at her Bangalore studio. They are for women who want to feel confident doing their own makeup.

What You Learn

  • Understanding your face shape, features and skin.
  • Choosing products that suit your routine and budget.
  • Shade matching, finishes and where to place products.
  • A balanced daily look you can do quickly.
  • Adapting makeup for weddings, festivals and parties.
  • Brows, eyes, lips and skin tone, step by step.

Who These Classes Are For

In particular, they suit beginners who feel unsure about products, and women who struggle to copy lessons. They also suit brides, their families and anyone who wants makeup that feels light.

Have a wedding coming up? Then combine the classes with bridal makeup in Bangalore for the main day.

Self-Grooming vs Professional Courses

These classes are about you. If you want to work on clients, choose a career course. For example, try the Professional Makeup Artist Launchpad or Bridal Makeup Artistry.

Common questions

Are these classes good for beginners?

Yes. They start with your own features and basic products, so you need no experience.

Do I need to bring my own makeup?

Bring your own products, so you learn to use what you have. You'll also learn what is worth buying.

Where are the classes held?

At Rekha's studio in the city, in small batches.
