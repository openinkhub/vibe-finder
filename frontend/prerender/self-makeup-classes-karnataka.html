Bridal Makeup, In Depth

The programme focuses entirely on the skills required to create professional bridal makeup. You learn bridal skin preparation, long-lasting and photography-ready complexion, bridal eye artistry, lashes, lips, face sculpting and complete bridal looks.

You will also learn to work with different skin tones, skin types, face shapes, eye shapes, ages and bridal aesthetics, while adapting your techniques to each bride.

What You Will Learn

  1. 01
    Bridal Skin Prep & Complexion
    Bridal skin preparation, skin types and concerns, priming, product compatibility, colour correction, foundation matching, natural to full-coverage bases, pigmentation and texture, long-wear techniques, setting and photography-friendly finishes.
  2. 02
    Bridal Face Artistry
    Concealing, highlighting, cream and powder contouring, face-shape-based sculpting, blush placement, bronzing, highlighting and creating dimension without heaviness.
  3. 03
    Bridal Eye Artistry
    Eye shapes, adaptation to individual eyes, bridal eyeshadow placement, soft bridal eyes, traditional bridal eyes, contemporary bridal glam, smokey eyes, shimmer and glitter, eyeliner, kohl and lower-lash detailing.
  4. 04
    Brows, Lashes & Details
    Bridal brow shaping, natural-looking brows, choosing lashes according to eye shape, lash trimming, strip lash application, layering, placement, mascara and finishing.
  5. 05
    Bridal Lip Artistry
    Lip preparation, lip shapes, pigmentation correction, lining and symmetry, overlining, bridal lip combinations, matte, satin and glossy finishes, long-lasting techniques and custom colour mixing.

The Bridal Look Lab

The emphasis throughout is adaptation because no two brides have the same face, skin or idea of beauty.

  • Classic Bridal
    Timeless, elegant and photograph-friendly.
  • Soft Glam Bridal
    Fresh, luminous and contemporary.
  • Traditional Bridal
    Stronger definition while complementing traditional bridal styling.
  • Modern Glam Bridal
    More defined eyes, sculpted complexion and elevated glamour.

Bridal Consultation

Before the makeup begins, you need to understand the bride. Training covers conducting a bridal consultation, understanding expectations, discussing outfit and jewellery, understanding the event, venue and lighting, recommending suitable makeup, managing references, setting realistic expectations, trial makeup etiquette and creating a personalised bridal look.

Bridal Longevity

A bridal look has to survive more than a photograph. The programme covers long-wear makeup, product layering, humidity, oil and sweat management, setting the complexion, maintaining skin-like texture, touch-up techniques and creating makeup that photographs beautifully while still looking natural in person.

Complete Bridal Makeup

Through guided hands-on practice, students bring skin preparation, correction, base, sculpting, blush, eyes, lashes, brows, lips, setting and final finishing together. The goal is to move from knowing individual steps to understanding the complete bridal look.

What Makes This Different?

We don't teach one bridal look. We teach you how to read the bride and create the right look for her.

A bride may have different skin, features, age, preferences, lighting, jewellery and expectations. The professional artist’s job is to know how to adapt. That is the skill the programme is designed to develop.
