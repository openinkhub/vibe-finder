About Rekha Krishnamurthy

Rekha is a makeup artist and teacher based in Bangalore. In fact, she has 14+ years in the trade and has styled 500+ brides, plus fashion and editorial looks across India. She works in HD and airbrush makeup, so she plans each look around your face, skin, style and event. Her kit includes trusted brands such as MAC and Bobbi Brown. She also keeps strict hygiene at each booking.

Which Course Is Right for You?

Start with your goal. If you want to do your own makeup well, choose the self grooming classes. They are short, hands-on and made for everyday life, work and parties.

If you want a career, look at the Professional Makeup Artist Launchpad. It runs for 120 hours across 20 sessions. On the other hand, if you already work as an artist and want to focus on brides, the Bridal Makeup Artistry course is a 60-hour bridal programme.

How Classes Are Taught

Every class mixes a demo with guided practice. So you watch Rekha work, then you try the same step yourself. She checks your work and shows you how to fix it.

Batches are small, usually about 15 to 20 students. As a result, each student gets time with Rekha. You also learn hygiene, kit building and how to choose products for your budget.

After the Course

Students who finish the professional course and final test receive a certificate. More importantly, they leave with a portfolio plan, pricing basics and a clear next step.

Batch dates are shared on Rekha's social media and on each course page. If you have a question, send a course request and Rekha will reply.

Common questions

Do I need any makeup experience to join?

No. The self grooming classes and the Launchpad course both start from the basics.

How long is the professional course?

The Professional Makeup Artist Launchpad runs for 120 hours across 20 sessions.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Students who complete the professional course and the final test receive a certificate of completion.

Is the course fee refundable?

No. Batches are small and seats are held for you, so the course fee is non-refundable.
