About the artist

14+ Years of Bridal Artistry & Expertise

For Rekha, great bridal makeup starts long before the brushes come out. First, it starts with understanding you: your features, your skin, your style and how you want to feel on your wedding day.

Based in Bengaluru and available for destination weddings, celebrations and fashion assignments across India, Rekha also offers something just as important: her complete attention. No double bookings. No rushing between brides. Your date is exclusively yours.

Her kit includes trusted brands such as MAC Cosmetics and Bobbi Brown. Also, she follows strict hygiene at each booking.

Why Brides Choose Rekha

Rekha has done bridal makeup for 14+ years. In that time, she has worked with 500+ brides from many regions and styles. So she knows how makeup behaves in heat, in long rituals and under strong flash.

She also plans each look with you. First, she asks about your skin, outfit, jewellery and the events. Then she suggests HD or airbrush makeup and a finish that suits you. As a result, you look like yourself, only polished and flash-proof.

Services for the Whole Wedding

Most weddings have more than one event. For this reason, Rekha plans looks for the engagement, haldi, mehendi, sangeet, wedding and reception. Each look can change in depth and colour, while the base stays steady.

She also does makeup for mothers, sisters and guests through non-bridal makeup. Grooms can book groom makeup for an even, natural finish on camera. In addition, party makeup covers birthdays, anniversaries and other events.

Learn Makeup With Rekha

Rekha also teaches. Her self grooming classes help you do your own makeup for work, parties and festivals. For new artists, the makeup courses cover skin, base, eyes, bridal looks, hair, draping and the business side.

Classes are hands-on, so you practise each step yourself. Batches stay small, and Rekha shares the venue when a new batch starts.

What the Big Day Looks Like

On the day, Rekha arrives early with her kit. First, she sets up near good light and a mirror. Then she preps your skin so the base sits well and lasts.

Next, she builds the base, shapes the brows and works on the eyes. After that come lashes, lips and a light set. If you booked hair and draping, she does those too, so the whole look comes together in one place.

She also leaves time for photos and small fixes. So you are not rushed, and you walk out calm. If your family has booked makeup too, she plans the order so the bride is ready first.

Finally, she checks the look in the room's light and in a phone photo. That last check helps the makeup look right both in person and in the album. Then you are ready to go.

First, send your date, venue and events. Then Rekha confirms if she is free and shares the plan and price. After that, a booking is confirmed once the advance is paid. Bridal trials are on offer on request as a paid service.

Good to know

Common questions

Q

How do I choose the best makeup artist in Bangalore for my wedding?

A

Look beyond the portfolio. The best makeup artist for you is the one who listens to what you want, understands your skin and features, and plans the look around your outfit, venue lighting and wedding schedule. Also ask about products, hygiene, HD or airbrush options and what the price includes. Rekha starts each booking with a consult, so you know exactly what to expect before the big day.

Q

How far in advance should I book?

A

For wedding-season dates (Oct–Feb), 4–6 months' notice keeps your date and trial slot secure. However, off-season dates can often be booked a few weeks ahead.

Q

HD vs. Airbrush makeup — what's the difference?

A

HD makeup uses light-reflecting, high-definition products applied by hand for a flawless close-up finish. Airbrush sprays a fine, even mist for an ultra-lightweight, long-wear base — ideal for full-day weddings and hot, humid weather.

Q

Do you travel for destination weddings?

A

Yes. Rekha travels across India for destination weddings and fashion assignments. Rekha shares travel and stay costs clearly at the consult.

Q

Do you offer a Trial?

A

Your trial is a detailed consult followed by a full-face makeup session using the same products and techniques planned for your wedding day. If you need it, Rekha can also style your hair.

This allows us to understand your preferences, refine the look and make sure everything feels right before your big day.

Bridal trials are available on request as a paid service.

Q

What courses do you offer?

A

Professional HD & bridal certification, an airbrush machine masterclass, and self-grooming & everyday makeup classes — all small-batch and hands-on at the Bangalore studio.

Q

What are the inclusions?

A

Most often a session of Makeup is inclusive of HD Makeup / Air Brush Makeup, Hair Styling, Outfit Styling (Saree/Lehenga Draping) at your venue. Any other specific requirements can be discussed at the time of consult.
