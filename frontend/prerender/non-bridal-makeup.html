How We Care About Your Desired Makeup

Your own idea of the look is the starting point. Tell Rekha how you want to look and what you often wear. Also say what feels at ease and what you don't want. Sample photos help show the finish you like. However, the look will not be an exact copy. Instead, Rekha adapts it to your features, skin, outfit, jewels, hair, the lighting and the day itself.

Do's and Don'ts

  • Share the event, outfit and expected ready time.
  • Explain whether you prefer light, natural, defined or glam makeup.
  • Discuss skin concerns and comfort needs.
  • Keep jewels and outfit available for coordination.
  • Allow enough time for hair and outfit styling where included.
  • Assume non-bridal means one standard makeup look.
  • Compare your makeup directly with a bride's makeup.
  • Experiment with new skincare at once before the event.
  • Leave hair and draping needs until the last minute.
  • Hide known skin sensitivities from the artist.

Care Before & After Your Makeup

Before: have the outfit, jewels and accessories ready. Communicate the event type and desired finish. If you need hair styling or draping, allow enough time in the timings.

After: avoid repeatedly touching the face or disturbing the hairstyle. Remove makeup fully after the event and return to your normal skincare routine.

Service Scope and Planning

Confirm the plan before the day. That means the event, the number of people, the place and the ready time. Also agree on outfit changes, waiting time and any extra styling. Also, if something isn't on the included list, agree on it before the booking.
