How Our Services Are Delivered

Rekha does all makeup in person, at the date, time and venue you agree. For example, this may be a home, a hall, a hotel or a shoot location. Similarly, she holds courses and classes at the venue she shares when a batch starts.

Sending a request does not confirm a booking. Your booking counts as confirmed only once you both agree the service, date, venue and price, and you pay any advance.

On the Day

So please be ready at the agreed time and place. Delays caused by the client may affect the quality or completeness of the service. Also, please share any skin allergies or sensitivities before the day.

Rekha sanitises tools, brushes and accessories before use. However, she may decline a booking if she cannot service the requested location. Full details are in the Terms & Conditions.

Changes and Rescheduling

To reschedule, ask at least 30 days ahead. Rekha will then check if the new date is free. One change is usually free; later changes may cost extra. See the refund policy for cancellations.

Common questions

Do you come to my venue?

Yes. Makeup is done in person at the agreed venue, such as your home, hall or hotel.

When is my booking confirmed?

Once you agree the service, date, venue and price, and you pay any advance.

Can I change my date?

Yes, with at least 30 days' notice and subject to open dates. One change is usually free.
