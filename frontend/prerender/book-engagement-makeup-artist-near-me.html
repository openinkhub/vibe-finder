Engagement Makeup Portfolio

Your engagement is a different celebration from your wedding, and your makeup can have its own personality. This portfolio showcases engagement makeup designed for photographs, celebrations and individual style. Explore engagement looks ranging from fresh and elegant finishes to soft glam and more defined occasion makeup.

Each look considers the person's features, outfit, jewellery, event setting and the level of makeup they feel comfortable wearing. Engagement makeup can sit between everyday makeup and full bridal styling. The aim is to create a polished look that works for the event and photographs well while allowing personal style to come through.

Explore the Work

Browse the portfolio to see different approaches to makeup, finish, styling and visual presentation. If you have a particular look in mind, use the images as references during your consultation rather than expecting an exact reproduction.

Plan Your Look

Your makeup can be discussed around the occasion, features, skin, outfit, jewellery, lighting, photography and the level of definition you are comfortable with. The goal is to create a considered look that fits the person and the purpose of the booking.

Good to know
What is different about engagement makeup?

Engagement makeup can be planned specifically for the engagement celebration rather than following the styling requirements of the wedding day.

Can engagement makeup be soft and natural?

Yes. The look can be adapted to your preferred level of coverage, definition and finish.

Can I use a portfolio image as a reference?

Yes. A reference can help communicate the style you like, after which the look can be adapted to your features.

Can my engagement and bridal looks be different?

Yes. They can be planned as separate looks according to your outfits, events and personal preferences.
