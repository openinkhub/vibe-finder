Is Makeup Your Passion?

Perhaps you have always been the person your friends ask to do their makeup. Perhaps you have spent hours watching tutorials, buying products and practising different looks. Or perhaps you have already started taking clients but still find yourself asking:

  • How do I work on different skin types?
  • What products should actually be in my kit?
  • How do I make makeup last?
  • How do I price myself?
  • How do I get my first real clients?
  • How do I turn this into a career?

The gap between loving makeup and working professionally as a makeup artist is much bigger than knowing which brush to use. You need technique, judgement, practice, confidence and an understanding of the business behind the brush. That is what this programme is designed to teach.

Welcome to Your Launchpad

Professional artistry, from the ground up.

Across 20 intensive sessions, you will learn how to approach makeup professionally rather than simply reproduce a look seen online. You will learn to analyse the individual in front of you, understand what their skin and features need, select products and techniques, create a look suited to the occasion and finish it to a professional standard.

The programme also takes you into client consultations, professional hygiene, kit building, pricing, branding, portfolio development, social media, client management and business fundamentals.

You will not just leave knowing more about makeup. You will leave knowing what to do with that knowledge.

The 120-Hour Professional Journey

20 sessions × 6 hours | 11 AM – 5 PM

  1. 01
    Phase 01 — Understand the Face
    Sessions 01–03: professional makeup artistry, facial anatomy, face shapes, proportions, feature analysis, eye and lip shapes, skin types, skin tones and undertones, Indian skin tones, client needs, skin preparation and colour correction.
  2. 02
    Phase 02 — Master the Base
    Sessions 04–06: foundation formulations, coverage and finishes, shade and undertone matching, customised shades, application techniques, pigmentation and texture, long-wear techniques, contouring, highlighting, bronzing, blush, glow and complexion finishing.
  3. 03
    Phase 03 — Eyes That Make the Look
    Sessions 07–10: eye anatomy and shapes, eyeshadow placement, blending, smokey eyes, smudged eyes, kohl, liner, glitter, shimmer, metallics, halo eyes, cut crease, brows, lashes and professional eye finishing.
  4. 04
    Phase 04 — Lips, Bridal & Occasion
    Sessions 11–15: lip preparation and correction, lining, symmetry, overlining, contouring, custom colour mixing, bridal complexion, bridal eyes, long-wear makeup, photography-friendly makeup, humidity and sweat management, complete bridal looks and occasion/party makeup.
  5. 05
    Phase 05 — Complete the Look
    Sessions 16–17: hair preparation, blow-drying, straightening, curling, waves, volume, basic party and bridal hairstyles, extensions, padding, accessories, saree draping, lehenga dupatta placement, head draping and coordination of makeup, hair, jewellery and outfit.
  6. 06
    Phase 06 — Become the Professional
    Sessions 18–20: professional kit building, hygiene, sourcing, pricing, packages, advance payments, cancellation policies, travel charges, client consultations, communication, personal branding, social media, portfolio development, networking, referrals and final assessment.

The Professional Difference

We don't teach you to copy a look. We teach you to understand why it works.

Every face is different. Every skin behaves differently. Every bride has a different idea of what makes her feel beautiful. A technique that works beautifully on one person may not work on another.

The programme therefore focuses on understanding, adapting and problem-solving rather than blindly following a step-by-step formula.

  • What does this skin need?
  • What does this face need?
  • What will photograph well?
  • What will last?
  • What will make my client feel like herself, only better?

Hands-On Learning, Not Just Handouts

Every session combines demonstration, explanation, guided practice and hands-on application. Students observe the technique, understand the reason behind product and technique choices, practise it themselves, identify what is not working, repeat the skill and receive individual feedback.

Personalised feedback is provided on strengths, technique and areas requiring further development.

The course ends with a plan — not simply a certificate.
