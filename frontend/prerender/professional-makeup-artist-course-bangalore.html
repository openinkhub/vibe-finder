Is Makeup Your Passion?

Perhaps you have always been the person your friends ask to do their makeup. Perhaps you have spent hours watching lessons, buying products and practising different looks. Or perhaps you have already started taking clients but still find yourself asking:

  • How do I work on different skin types?
  • What products should really be in my kit?
  • How do I make makeup last?
  • How do I price myself?
  • How do I get my first real clients?
  • How do I turn this into a career?

Loving makeup and working as a makeup artist are very different. The gap is much bigger than knowing which brush to use. You need method, judgement, practice, confidence and an understanding of the business behind the brush. That is exactly what this course teaches.

Welcome to Your Launchpad

Professional artistry, from the ground up.

Across 20 intensive sessions, you will learn how to approach makeup professionally rather than simply reproduce a look seen online. You learn to read the person in front of you and what their skin needs. Then you choose products, create a look for the event and finish it to an expert standard.

The course also covers consults, hygiene, kit building and pricing. In addition, it covers branding, your portfolio, social media and the basics of running a business.

You will not just leave knowing more about makeup. You will leave knowing what to do with that knowledge.

The 120-Hour Professional Journey

20 sessions × 6 hours | 11 AM – 5 PM

  1. 01
    Phase 01 — Know the Face
    Sessions 01–03: makeup artistry, facial anatomy, face shapes and features. Also skin types, skin tones and undertones, client needs, skin prep and colour correction.
  2. 02
    Phase 02 — Master the Base
    Sessions 04–06: foundation types, coverage, finishes and shade matching. Also long-wear methods, contour, highlight, bronzer, blush and finishing the base.
  3. 03
    Phase 03 — Eyes That Make the Look
    Sessions 07–10: eye shapes, eyeshadow placement and blending. Also smokey eyes, kohl, liner, shimmer, halo eyes, cut crease, brows and lashes.
  4. 04
    Phase 04 — Lips, Bridal & Occasion
    Sessions 11–15: lip prep, lining, overlining and custom colours. Also bridal skin and eyes, long-wear and flash-proof makeup, and full bridal and party looks.
  5. 05
    Phase 05 — Complete the Look
    Sessions 16–17: hair prep, blow-drying, curls, waves and basic party and bridal hairstyles. Also extensions, saree and dupatta draping, and bringing makeup, hair and outfit together.
  6. 06
    Phase 06 — Become the Professional
    Sessions 18–20: kit building, hygiene, pricing, packages and policies. Also consults, branding, social media, your portfolio, networking and the final assessment.

The Professional Difference

We don't teach you to copy a look. We teach you to understand why it works.

Each face is different. Each skin behaves differently. And each bride has her own idea of what makes her feel lovely. A method that works beautifully on one person may not work on another.

The programme therefore focuses on understanding, adapting and problem-solving rather than blindly following a step-by-step formula.

  • What does this skin need?
  • What does this face need?
  • What will photograph well?
  • What will last?
  • What will make my client feel like herself, only better?

Hands-On Learning, Not Just Handouts

Each session combines demonstration, explanation, guided practice and hands-on application. First, students watch the method and learn why it works. Then they practise it, spot what isn't working and repeat it, with personal feedback.

The course shows you what to buy first and what can wait. It also shows where to save and how to build a flexible shade range. You learn to build a kit that fits your budget, clients and career, instead of buying everything you see online.
