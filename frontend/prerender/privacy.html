This policy is from Rekha Krishnamurthy / Makeup by Rekha Krishnamurthy ("we", "us", "our"). We run the website https://rekhakrishnamurthy.com/ (the "Site"). We offer makeup services (bridal, party, editorial and fashion), makeup courses and personal shopping (together, the "Services"). This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal data when you visit the Site, send a request or book with us.

By using the Site or sending your details through our forms, you agree to this policy.

1.1 Information We Collect

When you fill in a request or contact form, book a consult or join a course, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, email, phone number, city and country.
  • Booking details: the service you want (for example bridal, engagement, party, editorial, fashion or a course), your dates, your venue and any notes you share.
  • Messages: what you send us by WhatsApp, phone, email, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, Pinterest or any other channel listed on the Site.
  • Payment details: if you pay an advance, we (or our payment partner) may handle the payment details. We do not keep full card or bank details on our own servers.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser, device and the pages you visit. Cookies and tools such as Google Tag Manager also collect this on their own.

We do not try to collect sensitive data, such as health details. The only exception is what you choose to tell us, for example a skin allergy that affects which products we use.

1.2 How We Use Your Information

We use your details to:

  • Reply to your request and confirm bookings for makeup, courses or personal shopping.
  • Contact you about your booking, timing, changes and payments.
  • Improve the Site, our Services and your experience.
  • Send news, offers or course updates, unless you have opted out.
  • Meet legal, tax and accounting rules.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

1.3 Sharing of Information

In some cases, your details may go to:

  • Service providers who help us run the Site, take payments, send messages or manage bookings. Examples are hosting firms, payment gateways and email, SMS or WhatsApp providers. They must keep your data private.
  • Team members and helpers who work on your booking, such as an assisting makeup artist or hairstylist.
  • Legal authorities, when the law or a court requires it, or to protect our rights, safety or property.

Without your consent, we do not give your data to others for their own marketing.

1.4 Cookies & Tracking

The Site may use cookies and similar tools, including Google Tag Manager and other analytics tools. They show us how people use the Site, so we can make it work better. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings. However, some parts of the Site may not work as intended if you do.

1.5 Data Retention

We keep your personal data only as long as we need it for the reasons in this policy. That includes keeping booking records, meeting legal and tax rules and settling disputes. After that, we delete it safely or make it anonymous.

1.6 Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your data safe from misuse, change, leaks or loss. Still, no way of sending data over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot promise complete security.

1.7 Your Rights

Subject to the law, you can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your data, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Stop sending you marketing at any time. For example, reply "unsubscribe" or contact us directly.

To use these rights, email contact@rekhakrishnamurthy.com or call +91-98454 80507.

1.8 Children's Privacy

Our Services are for people aged 18 or over. A minor can use them only with a parent or guardian involved, for example for party makeup at a minor's event. We do not knowingly collect a child's personal data without that involvement.

1.9 Third-Party Links

The Site may link to other platforms, such as Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, Pinterest, YouTube, WhatsApp and Google Maps or Reviews. However, those sites have their own privacy rules, and we are not responsible for them. So please read their privacy policies.

1.10 Changes to This Policy

This policy may change from time to time. When it does, the new version will appear on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If you keep using the Site after a change, you accept the new policy.
