Rekha Krishnamurthy / Makeup by Rekha Krishnamurthy ("we", "us", "our") operates the website https://rekhakrishnamurthy.com/ (the "Site") and provides makeup artistry, bridal/party/editorial/fashion makeup services, makeup training courses, and personal shopping services (collectively, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit the Site, submit an enquiry, or book our Services.

By using the Site or submitting your details through our enquiry/contact forms, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

1.1 Information We Collect

When you fill out our "Enquire Now" or contact forms, book a consultation, or register for a course, we may collect:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number, city and country.
  • Booking details: type of service requested (e.g., bridal, engagement, party, editorial, fashion makeup, courses), preferred dates, venue/location, and any comments or preferences you share.
  • Communication data: messages sent via WhatsApp, phone calls, email, Instagram/Facebook/Twitter/Pinterest, or any other channel listed on the Site.
  • Payment-related information: if you make an advance/booking payment, we (or our payment partner) may process transaction details; we do not store full card or banking credentials on our own servers.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected automatically through cookies and analytics/tracking tools (e.g., Google Tag Manager).

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data (such as health information) beyond what you voluntarily share with us (for example, a skin allergy relevant to product selection).

1.2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to enquiries and confirm bookings for makeup services, courses, or personal shopping.
  • Communicate with you about appointments, scheduling, changes, and payments.
  • Improve our Site, Services, and customer experience.
  • Send promotional updates, offers, or course information, where you have not opted out.
  • Comply with legal, accounting, or regulatory needs.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

1.3 Sharing of Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist us in operating the Site, processing payments, sending communications, or managing bookings (e.g., hosting providers, payment gateways, email/SMS/WhatsApp service providers), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Team members and assistants engaged for your specific booking (e.g., an assisting makeup artist or hairstylist for your event).
  • Legal authorities, where required by law, court order, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

We do not share your data with third parties for their independent marketing purposes without your consent.

1.4 Cookies & Tracking

The Site may use cookies and similar technologies (including Google Tag Manager and related analytics tools) to understand how visitors use the Site and to improve functionality. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; some parts of the Site may not function as intended if you do so.

1.5 Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, including maintaining booking records, complying with legal/tax obligations, and resolving disputes, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

1.6 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

1.7 Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to our legal/record-keeping obligations.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time (e.g., by replying "unsubscribe" or contacting us directly).

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@rekhakrishnamurthy.com or +91-98454 80507.

1.8 Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old, or minors acting with the involvement/consent of a parent or guardian (e.g., for engagement/party makeup for a minor's event). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without such involvement.

1.9 Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party platforms (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, Pinterest, YouTube, WhatsApp, Google Maps/Reviews). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. Please review their respective privacy policies.

1.10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
