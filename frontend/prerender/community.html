Common questions

What is Rekha's bridal makeup price?

HD Bridal Makeup is ₹30,000 per session and Airbrush Bridal Makeup is ₹40,000 per session.

How much does engagement makeup cost with Rekha?

For engagements, however, HD Makeup is ₹20,000 per session and Airbrush is ₹30,000 per session.

What is the starting price for non-bridal makeup?

Also, HD Non-Bridal Makeup starts from ₹10,000 per person per session.

How much is party makeup?

Party Makeup in HD starts from ₹8,000, with makeup and a simple hairstyle. You can also add extras.

What is the groom makeup price?

HD Groom Makeup is ₹5,000 per session and includes makeup only.

How much does fashion makeup cost?

Fashion Makeup for portfolio shoots starts from ₹10,000 onwards, with customisation available.

What is the price of editorial makeup?

Editorial Makeup is price on request, with customisation available.
