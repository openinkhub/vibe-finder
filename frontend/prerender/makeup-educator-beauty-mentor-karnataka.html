Choosing a Makeup Educator and Beauty Mentor in Karnataka

How to choose a makeup educator and beauty mentor in Karnataka, whether you are learning for yourself or for a career.

By Rekha Krishnamurthy · 19 November 2026 · 3 min read

Why the Educator Matters

Whether you want to become a professional makeup artist or simply do your own makeup well, who teaches you matters. A good makeup educator and beauty mentor in Karnataka combines real client experience with patient, practical teaching.

Rekha Krishnamurthy has worked as a bridal makeup artist for 14 years and teaches at her Bangalore studio. You can see her bridal work at bridal makeup in Bangalore.

What to Look For in a Makeup Educator

  • Active professional work, not only teaching.
  • Small batches with hands-on practice on real faces.
  • Clear feedback and correction during practice.
  • A curriculum that covers skin, products, hygiene and client handling.
  • Ongoing mentorship after the course.

Choose the Right Programme

For yourself: the self-grooming classes help you understand your features and build an everyday and occasion routine.

For a career: the Professional Makeup Artist Launchpad is a 120-hour programme covering makeup, bridal, occasion looks, hair, draping and professional development.

For bridal specialists: the Bridal Makeup Artistry programme is a focused 60-hour bridal course.

Learning in Small Batches

Small batches mean each learner practises with guidance and gets direct feedback. See all options on the makeup courses page.
