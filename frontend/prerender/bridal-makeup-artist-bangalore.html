Designed Around Your Occasion and Your Desired Look

Your desired makeup matters. If you prefer soft, natural, defined, glamorous, traditional or contemporary makeup, that preference should be discussed before the final look is decided. Reference images are useful for communicating direction, but the finished makeup needs to be adapted to your own face, skin, outfit, jewellery, lighting and photography. Rekha's role is to guide you professionally without losing your identity.

Why This Service Matters

Bridal makeup has to work through a long and emotionally important day. You may move from preparation to rituals, photographs, greetings, travel, reception or several hours of celebration. The makeup therefore needs thoughtful skin preparation, complexion balance, eye and lip definition, lashes, hairstyle and outfit styling.

How We Care About Your Desired Makeup

Your preferred makeup direction should be the starting point. Tell Rekha what you want to look like, what you normally wear, what you are comfortable with and what you do not want. Reference photographs can help communicate a finish, but they are not a promise that an identical look should be copied. The final application is adapted to your facial features, skin, outfit, jewellery, hairstyle, lighting, photography and the practical demands of the occasion.

Do's and Don'ts

  • Share your wedding schedule, venue, ready time, outfit and jewellery details as early as possible.
  • Bring or share clear reference images that explain the makeup direction you like.
  • Discuss skin concerns, sensitivities and previous makeup experiences before the appointment.
  • Allow enough preparation time for makeup, hair, draping, photography and final checks.
  • Keep the final look aligned with your personality rather than choosing a trend only because it is popular.
  • Arrive with an unrealistic schedule that leaves no time for hair, draping or final checks.
  • Make major skincare experiments immediately before the wedding.
  • Assume that a reference photograph will look identical on every face.
  • Add unplanned outfit changes or waiting requirements without discussing them in advance.
  • Judge the final look only from a close mirror view; consider the complete outfit, lighting and photography.

Care Before & After Your Makeup

Before: organise the outfit, jewellery, accessories and preparation space. Skin should be prepared according to your established routine rather than through last-minute experiments. Hair should be clean and ready according to the agreed styling plan. The wedding schedule should clearly identify when you must be fully ready, when photographs begin and whether any travel is required.

After: avoid unnecessary touching of the face and allow the products and styling to settle. Follow the artist's practical guidance for maintaining the look during the event. After the event, remove makeup thoroughly and return to a gentle skincare routine rather than sleeping in makeup.
