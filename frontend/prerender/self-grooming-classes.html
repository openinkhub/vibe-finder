Makeup Should Work For You

You don't need lots of products or a long routine to feel confident with makeup. Instead, you need to know your own face, your skin and the techniques that suit you.

Rekha built her self grooming classes on that idea. You won't learn to copy one look. You'll learn how your face works, so you can make better makeup choices for yourself.

What You Can Learn

  • Understanding your face shape and key features
  • Knowing your skin and how to prep it
  • Choosing products that make sense for your routine
  • Understanding shade, finish and product placement
  • Creating a balanced everyday makeup look
  • Adapting makeup for occasions and personal preferences
  • Improving eye, brow, lip and complexion application
  • Using makeup without making it look heavy
  • Building confidence through guided practice
  • Learning what works for you instead of blindly following trends

A Practical, Personal Learning Approach

  1. 01
    Understand
    Start with your own features, skin, preferences and makeup goals.
  2. 02
    Learn
    Learn what each product and technique does, instead of memorising a routine.
  3. 03
    Practise
    Apply the techniques with guidance so you can see what works on your face.
  4. 04
    Refine
    Spot what needs fixing and learn how to balance the look.
  5. 05
    Repeat
    Build confidence by practising techniques you can use in real life.

Who Is This Workshop For?

  • Women who want to improve their everyday makeup
  • Beginners who feel unsure about products or application
  • Women preparing for weddings, celebrations or special occasions
  • Women who have watched tutorials but struggle to recreate the result
  • Anyone who wants practical tips, not trend rules

What Makes the Approach Different?

Makeup has no rules. If you like it, wear it.

Not everyone needs the same makeup routine. After all, your face, skin, comfort and style are your own. The classes help you see what makes you different, and choose what feels natural and easy for you.
