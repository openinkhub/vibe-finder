Last updated: November 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy covers bookings with Makeup by Rekha Krishnamurthy. In particular, it applies to makeup (Bridal, Engagement, Non-Bridal, Party, Editorial and Fashion), Makeup Courses and Personal Shopping. It also applies whether you book through https://rekhakrishnamurthy.com/ or directly by phone, WhatsApp, email or social media.

3.1 Booking Confirmation & Advance Payment

  • Your booking is confirmed only once we receive the advance we told you about when you asked.
  • Then the advance holds your date and time slot for you alone, because we do not double-book.

3.2 Cancellations by the Client

  • First, you must cancel in writing, by email, WhatsApp or a message on the Site. So the cancellation counts from the date and time we receive it.
  • 30 days or more before the service date: we do not refund the advance. However, you can move it to a future date, if that date is free.
  • Less than 30 days before the service date: we do not refund the advance, because we cannot offer the slot to another client at short notice.
  • No-show or cancelling on the event day: we give no refund, and you may still owe any balance.
  • Makeup courses: if you cancel 30 days before the course starts, you may get a partial refund, minus an admin fee. After the course starts, fees are usually non-refundable, unless we decide otherwise.

3.3 Cancellations or Changes by Us

  • Rarely, we may need to cancel or move a confirmed booking for reasons outside our control, such as illness, an emergency or force majeure. In that case, we will tell you as soon as we can. Then you can choose either (a) a full refund of what you paid, or (b) a new date that suits us both.
  • Also, with your consent, we will try to arrange a qualified artist from our team to step in, where we can.

3.4 Rescheduling

  • To reschedule, ask at least 30 days ahead. However, the new date depends on whether we are free.
  • Also, you can usually reschedule once for free. After that, changes may cost extra.

3.5 Refund Process

  • First, we approve the refund. Then we pay it back to the way you paid, within 7–14 business days of approval.
  • However, bank and payment times may vary, and we cannot control them.

3.6 Service-Related Issues

  • If you are unhappy with a service, please tell us within 24–48 hours of the event. Then we can review it and, where fair, offer a fix. For example, that may be a partial adjustment, a credit for a future service or another remedy, at our discretion. However, a refund is not automatic when a finished, expert service simply did not match personal taste.

3.7 Non-Refundable Items

  • Some costs are usually non-refundable once spent. For example, these include travel charges, trial fees (if paid separately and the trial has taken place) and money paid to others for you. For example, that includes products bought for your event through Personal Shopping.

3.8 Contact for Cancellations/Refunds

To cancel, reschedule or ask about a refund, contact us at: 📞 +91-98454 80507 ✉️ contact@rekhakrishnamurthy.com

Common questions

Can I reschedule my booking?

Yes, but ask at least 30 days ahead, subject to open dates. Also, one change is usually free; later changes may cost extra.

Is the advance refundable?

In short, section 3.2 of this policy explains what happens to the advance when a client cancels.

Whom do I contact about a refund?

First, use the contact details in section 3.8 of this policy. Or reach Rekha's makeup studio in Bangalore through the contact page.
