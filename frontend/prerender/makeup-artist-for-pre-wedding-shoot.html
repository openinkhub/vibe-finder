Photo Shoot Makeup Portfolio

Makeup for a shoot needs to work for both the person and the camera. This portfolio showcases makeup created for pre-wedding, portrait, fashion, editorial and other image-focused shoots. The approach looks at the purpose of the shoot, the lighting, styling and outfit. It also looks at the camera and the look the subject wants.

Depending on the project, makeup can range from fresh and natural-looking to polished glam or more creative styling. Photoshoot makeup is not simply about applying more makeup. It is about choosing the right base, definition and finish for the camera. At the same time, the subject stays comfortable and still looks like herself.

Explore the Work

Browse the portfolio to see different looks, finishes and styling. If you like a look, bring it to your consult as a sample. However, it will not be an exact copy.

Plan Your Look

At the consult, talk through the event, your skin, outfit and jewels. Then decide how bold you want to go. Overall, the goal is a look that suits you and the event.

Common questions

What type of shoot makeup is offered?

The portfolio can represent different image-focused work such as pre-wedding, portrait, fashion and editorial-style shoots.

Makeup That Works on Location

Pre-wedding shoots move from sun to shade and from one outfit to the next. So these looks use long-wear products and a base that copes with heat and wind. Look for skin that still looks even in strong sunlight.

Also notice how the look changes between outfits. A western dress may get a softer, fresh face, while a saree look gets more depth. Quick touch-ups keep the couple ready for each new frame.

Planning Your Shoot Day

Share the location, the number of outfits and the light you expect. Then Rekha plans the looks and the touch-ups. To book, send a shoot request or see photoshoot makeup.
