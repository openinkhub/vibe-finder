Pre-Wedding Makeup for Every Function

How a pre-wedding makeup artist plans looks for the engagement, haldi, mehendi, sangeet and shoot.

By Rekha Krishnamurthy · 19 November 2026 · 3 min read

Makeup for Each Pre-Wedding Function

Before the wedding come the engagement, haldi, mehendi, sangeet and photo shoots. Each one also has its own mood, outfit and lighting. So a pre-wedding makeup artist plans fresh looks that don't outshine the wedding day.

So here is how to approach each function. Rekha offers bridal makeup in Bangalore for the wedding and each event before it.

Looks by Function

  • Engagement
    Polished and photogenic: often the first formal photos of the couple.
  • Haldi
    Light, fresh and waterproof, with minimal base that survives the turmeric.
  • Mehendi
    Bright and playful, matching colourful outfits and daytime light.
  • Sangeet
    Evening glam with more definition for dancing and stage lights.
  • Pre-wedding shoot
    Natural, flash-proof makeup that works outdoors and in motion.

Keeping the Wedding Look Special

Plan the pre-wedding looks after you decide the bridal look. That way each event has its own feel, and the wedding day remains the most complete version of you.

For engagement looks, see engagement makeup. For shoot looks, see shoot makeup. Brides in South Bangalore can see pre-wedding makeup in Banashankari.

Do Some Functions Yourself

For smaller events like a mehendi at home, many brides prefer to do their own makeup. The self-grooming classes show you how.

Common questions

Do I need a makeup artist for haldi?

Many brides keep haldi simple. If photos matter, a light, waterproof look from an expert helps.

Should my engagement look match my wedding look?

It works best when the looks are related but different, so the wedding look still feels special.

Can I book one artist for all pre-wedding events?

Yes. Booking one artist makes it easier to plan looks that work together.
