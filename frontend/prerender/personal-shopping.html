Why Personal Makeup Shopping?

You do not need to buy each product that is trending. You need to know which products solve your needs and which choices make sense for your makeup routine.

This consult makes shopping more focused. First, you work out your needs. Then you choose the right products with expert help, instead of trial and error.

What the Consultation Can Cover

  • Understanding your current makeup collection
  • Identifying what you really need
  • Planning daily and event makeup essentials
  • Understanding right product categories and finishes
  • Choosing skin tone products according to your needs
  • Understanding shade and undertone considerations
  • Reviewing products you already own
  • Identifying needless duplication
  • Prioritising purchases according to your budget
  • Creating a more hands-on and versatile makeup collection

A Simple Shopping Process

  1. 01
    Know
    Discuss your makeup routine, preferences, skin needs and goals.
  2. 02
    Review
    Look at what you already own and spot helpful products, gaps and duplication.
  3. 03

    Separate essential purchases from products that can wait.
  4. 04

  5. 05
    Build
    Create a hands-on collection that fits your routine and budget.

Who Is This For?

  • Women starting their makeup collection
  • Women who have too many products but still feel unsure what to use
  • Anyone struggling with foundation or skin tone choices
  • Women preparing for a wedding, event or lifestyle change
  • Anyone wanting to make better use of an existing makeup kit
  • Women who want expert guidance before making a larger makeup buy

The Goal Is Not More Products

A helpful makeup collection is not necessarily a large one. The goal is to know what you need, why you need it and how it fits into your routine. As a result, you make hands-on choices, not ones driven by trends.
