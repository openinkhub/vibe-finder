Why Personal Makeup Shopping?

You do not need to buy every product that is trending. You need to understand which products solve your needs and which choices make sense for your makeup routine.

This consultation is designed to make shopping more intentional: understand your needs, identify suitable product categories and make decisions with professional guidance instead of buying through trial and error.

What the Consultation Can Cover

  • Understanding your current makeup collection
  • Identifying what you actually need
  • Planning everyday and occasion makeup essentials
  • Understanding suitable product categories and finishes
  • Choosing complexion products according to your needs
  • Understanding shade and undertone considerations
  • Reviewing products you already own
  • Identifying unnecessary duplication
  • Prioritising purchases according to your budget
  • Creating a more practical and versatile makeup collection

A Simple Shopping Process

  1. 01
    Understand
    Discuss your makeup routine, preferences, skin needs and goals.
  2. 02
    Review
    Look at what you already own and identify useful products, gaps and duplication.
  3. 03
    Prioritise
    Separate essential purchases from products that can wait.
  4. 04
    Choose
    Use professional guidance to narrow down suitable products, shades and finishes.
  5. 05
    Build
    Create a practical collection that fits your routine and budget.

Who Is This For?

  • Women starting their makeup collection
  • Women who have too many products but still feel unsure what to use
  • Anyone struggling with foundation or complexion choices
  • Women preparing for a wedding, event or lifestyle change
  • Anyone wanting to make better use of an existing makeup kit
  • Women who want professional guidance before making a larger makeup purchase

The Goal Is Not More Products

A useful makeup collection is not necessarily a large one. The goal is to understand what you need, why you need it and how it fits into your routine. The consultation helps you make decisions that are practical rather than driven only by trends.
