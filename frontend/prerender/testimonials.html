What Our Happy Clients Are Saying

Across weddings, engagements, shoots, and workshops, one thread runs through almost every review: clients feel heard before they feel made-up. They talk about waking up calm on their big day. They also talk about a face that still looked like them and a session that never felt rushed.

What Matters Most to Them

  • Feeling heard before the brush touches their skin
  • A natural-feeling finish that still holds up in photos and through the day
  • Punctuality and a calm, unhurried studio or on-site experience
  • Clear communication about timing, pricing, and what's included
  • Feeling comfortable enough to ask questions or request changes
  • Makeup and hair that stays put through rituals, dancing, and long photo sessions

How They're Referring Us Today

Most new requests now arrive through a friend, cousin, or colleague who was a guest at a wedding and asked, "who did your makeup?" Brides tag Rekha in their wedding reels, and many of those looks appear in the portfolio. Mothers pass her number to their daughters. And whole friend groups book together after one good experience. Referrals, not advertising, are the biggest source of new bookings.

Clients Who Keep Coming Back

Repeat bookings are common. A client who books engagement makeup often returns for her wedding. Then she comes back again for a sister's or cousin's wedding. Some clients first join a self-makeup class. Later, they return before a big event and ask Rekha to do their makeup. Others rebook each year for a birthday, anniversary or festive party. In short, the bond lasts well beyond the first event.

About Rekha Krishnamurthy

Rekha is a makeup artist and teacher based in Bangalore. In fact, she has 14+ years in the trade and has styled 500+ brides, plus fashion and editorial looks across India. She works in HD and airbrush makeup, so she plans each look around your face, skin, style and event. Her kit includes trusted brands such as MAC and Bobbi Brown. She also keeps strict hygiene at each booking.
