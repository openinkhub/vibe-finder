What Our Happy Clients Are Saying

Across weddings, engagements, photoshoots, and workshops, one thread runs through almost every review: clients feel heard before they feel made-up. They talk about waking up on their big day without anxiety, seeing a face in the mirror that still looks like them, and having a makeup experience that felt calm rather than rushed.

What Matters Most to Them

  • Being listened to before the brush ever touches their skin
  • A natural-feeling finish that still holds up on camera and through the day
  • Punctuality and a calm, unhurried studio or on-location experience
  • Clear communication about timing, pricing, and what's included
  • Feeling comfortable enough to ask questions or request changes
  • Makeup and hair that stays put through rituals, dancing, and long photo sessions

How They're Referring Us Today

Most new enquiries now arrive through a friend, cousin, or colleague who was a guest at a wedding and asked, "who did your makeup?" Brides tag Rekha in their wedding reels and reception photos, mothers pass the number along to their daughters, and entire friend groups book their engagement and party makeup together after one of them has a good experience. Referrals, not advertising, are the biggest source of new bookings.

Clients Who Keep Coming Back

Repeat bookings are common. A client who booked her engagement makeup often returns months later for her wedding, and again the following year for a sister's or cousin's wedding. Some clients who first came in for a self-makeup workshop return before a big event asking Rekha to do their makeup in person. Others simply rebook every year for a birthday, anniversary, or festive party — the relationship outlasts the first event it was built for.

About Rekha Krishnamurthy

Rekha Krishnamurthy is a Bangalore-based professional makeup artist and educator with 14 years of hands-on experience and Hundreds of bridal, editorial and fashion assignments across India. Specialising in HD and Airbrush makeup, Rekha takes a personalised approach to every look, tailoring the makeup to each client's features, skin, style and occasion. Her professional kit includes products from leading international beauty brands such as MAC Cosmetics, Bobbi Brown, Estée Lauder, HUDA Beauty, Lancôme, Urban Decay and Anastasia Beverly Hills. Every assignment follows strict hygiene and professional makeup practices, with careful attention to product selection, skin preparation and application.
