Hiring a Wedding Makeup Artist: What to Expect

What a wedding makeup artist does for the bride, the family and the groom, and how to plan the morning.

By Rekha Krishnamurthy · 19 November 2026 · 3 min read

What a Wedding Makeup Artist Does

A wedding makeup artist does more than the bride's face. Depending on what you book, they can cover the bride's looks for each function. They can also do the mother and sisters, the groom, hair and draping, all on one timings.

Here is what to expect when you hire a makeup artist, so the day runs smoothly. Rekha's bridal makeup in Bangalore covers the full wedding experience.

Who Often Needs Makeup

  • The bride, for each function.
  • The bride's mother, sisters and close family.
  • The groom, for natural, flash-proof grooming.
  • Bridesmaids or friends who want a coordinated look.

Planning Makeup for the Whole Family

When a few people need makeup on the same morning, the timing matters. Rekha plans the bride's look first. Then she fits family makeup around the ready time. So share the number of people and the timings early. That way, Rekha can confirm the right team and time.

For family and guests, see non-bridal makeup. For grooms, see groom makeup.

On the Wedding Day

Keep a quiet, well-lit room ready, wear a front-opening top, and have your outfit and jewels laid out. Skin should be clean and moisturised. The artist will handle the rest.

Brides in East Bangalore can see wedding makeup in Indiranagar. For your own daily makeup after the wedding, try the self-grooming classes.

Common questions

Can a makeup artist do makeup for my family too?

Yes. Family and guests can book with the bride, and Rekha schedules them around her ready time.

How many people can Rekha do on the wedding morning?

It depends on the ready time and the looks requested. Share the numbers early, so Rekha can plan the timings.

Is hair styling included with wedding makeup?

Yes. Rekha's bridal makeup includes hair styling, lashes, extensions as needed and draping.
