**Why this matters:** Google's quality rater guidelines explicitly look for *who created this content and are they a demonstrated expert*. A bio with a real name, verifiable experience, and links to social proof is the single strongest E-E-A-T signal a small local business site can add.

### 2. Trust Signals to Add Site-Wide
- **Verifiable numbers**: Replace vague phrases like "hundreds of clients" with a specific, honest figure — e.g., "**320+ brides styled since 2016**" — and keep it updated. Specific numbers outperform vague superlatives for both trust and search.
- **Review attribution**: Your testimonials currently show first name only (Harshita Naik, Rumini Shesha, Nandini). Where possible, link to the actual Google Review so it's independently verifiable — this is a major trust signal search engines and users both respond to.
- **Visible policies**: Link to your Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and Refund & Cancellation Policy in the footer of every page (already drafted separately) — sites with clear policies score materially higher on trustworthiness.
- **Schema markup** (technical, ask your developer): Add `Person` schema for Rekha, `LocalBusiness`/`ProfessionalService` schema for the business, and `Review`/`AggregateRating` schema wrapping your testimonials. This gives search engines machine-readable proof of your experience and trust signals — it doesn't change what users see, but it strengthens how Google evaluates the page.
- **"Last updated" or process transparency**: A short line like *"Every bridal package includes an in-person or video consultation before the big day"* signals a real, considered process rather than templated content.

### 3. Experience Signals to Weave into Copy
Google's "Experience" signal specifically rewards content that reads like it was written by someone who has *actually done the thing* — not generic marketing copy. Wherever possible, replace generic claims with specific, sensory, first-hand detail (before/after skin routines, a real anecdote from a shoot, a specific product's use-case). Examples are built into each page draft below.
